What a battery does for you

Three reasons people add a battery.

01

Backup when the grid goes down

A plain on-grid inverter switches off for safety whenever PEA power fails, even in full sun. A hybrid inverter with a battery and a backup circuit keeps your essential loads running: lights, fridge, Wi-Fi, pumps, a room air con.

02

Use your solar at night

Thailand pays only a low fixed rate for power you export. Storing the midday surplus and using it in the evening is usually worth more than sending it to the grid.

03

Power where there is no grid

Mountain homestays, farms and remote sites run fully off-grid on solar and batteries, with a generator connection through an ATS for long cloudy spells.

How we design it

Sized from your real consumption, not the biggest box on offer.

  1. 01
    Read your consumption

    We look at your bills and, where possible, meter or inverter data to see how much you use in the evening and at night, and your peak loads.

  2. 02
    Choose what must stay on

    We agree with you which circuits go on the backup panel, so the battery protects what matters instead of draining on everything.

  3. 03
    Match battery and inverter

    Battery size comes from your night-time load; the pairing is checked against the inverter maker's approved battery list, so charging, backup and warranty all work as they should.

  4. 04
    Install it safely

    Batteries go in shade, ventilated and off the floor, each with its own DC breaker, copper cabling and proper earthing.

  5. 05
    Commission and hand over

    We test the backup switch-over, set up the monitoring app on your phone, handle the PEA connection paperwork and show you how the system works.

Good to know

Battery questions.

Can I add a battery to my existing solar system?

If you already have a hybrid inverter, usually yes: we check that the battery is on the inverter maker's approved list. With a plain on-grid inverter, the inverter normally has to be replaced with a hybrid one or a separate battery inverter added. We will tell you which after a site check.

Does a system with a battery qualify for the ฿200,000 tax deduction?

An on-grid rooftop system with a battery can qualify, as long as it is a new system of up to 10 kWp connected to PEA or MEA between 3 March 2026 and 31 December 2028. You need a full e-Tax Invoice, which Solar ESS issues for every system.

Are lithium batteries safe in a house?

We fit LiFePO4 only, the most stable lithium chemistry for homes. Each battery gets its own DC breaker, is installed in shade with ventilation and off the floor, and is wired in copper with proper earthing.

Can I connect a generator?

Yes. We recommend the generator size and provide the connection point through an ATS. The generator supplier installs and warrants the generator, and we connect it to the solar system together with their team.

How much does a battery system cost?

It depends on the battery size, the inverter and how much of your home or business must stay on. The battery is priced on top of the solar system. Book a consultation or message us on LINE and we will send a quotation.
