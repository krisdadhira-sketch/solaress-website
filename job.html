Our standard for every job

Premium materials onlyEvery quote must propose premium materials: copper conductors, cable runs in IMC or EMT conduit, and well-matched electrical parts, with breakers, cables, fuses and SPDs rated for each other and for the load.
Qualified, certified electriciansThe work must be performed by a qualified electrician holding valid certificates. Attach copies of your certificates when you apply or quote.

Applications and quotes that do not meet this standard are not accepted.

Solar ESS stands in the middle, for both sides

This page is the central place where solar jobs for installers are published. Solar ESS acts as the mediator between employers and installers. Members can also post their own jobs here for other installers to take on, and look for candidates. Any communication or business agreement made outside the Solar ESS platform is not within Solar ESS’s capacity to manage, and we cannot support either party in a case of fraud or cheating. Post a job →

  • Real jobs, fair pay: we check that every job published on our site is real and pays installers a fair amount.
  • Real installers: we make sure installers are genuine, professional and skilled, and do not take advantage of employers.
