Inverter

Solis hybrid inverters

Also fitted where the project calls for it: Huawei, Sigenergy, SolarEdge and ATMOCE.

The inverter is the part most likely to need attention during the life of a system. We pick on service first and price second.

Why we pick it

  • A 10-year standard warranty on the hybrid range.
  • Support that answers. When something needs checking, Solis technical staff respond to us directly, so a fault is not left waiting.
  • Room to grow: the hybrid models accept far more panel capacity than their rated output, so the array can be sized generously.
  • Dependable in backup and off-grid use, which matters at remote sites in the North.

Solar panels

LONGi Hi-MO X10 back-contact modules

Tier-1 manufacturer. We also install Aiko Neostar back-contact modules.

Panels sit on the roof for decades. We choose the maker we expect to still be there to honour the warranty.

Why we pick it

  • Back-contact cells put the wiring behind the cell, so nothing on the front blocks the light.
  • Better behaviour when part of a panel is shaded by a tree, a pole or a neighbouring roof.
  • Long cover: a product warranty plus a 25 to 30 year power-output warranty, with exact terms stated on your quotation.
  • High output per panel means fewer panels, fewer roof fixings and less cable for the same system size.

Breakers

CHINT MCB and MCCB

Sized to the cable and the inverter on every circuit, DC and AC.

A breaker is cheap until the day it has to work. This is the wrong place to save money.

Why we pick it

  • Each breaker is matched to the cable it protects, so the breaker trips before the cable overheats.
  • DC circuits get breakers rated for DC. An AC breaker on a DC string cannot be relied on to break the current.
  • Certified to IEC standards and stocked in Thailand, so a replacement is available quickly.
  • Labelled and laid out in the box so the next electrician can see what each one does.

Surge protection (SPD)

Type II surge protection on both the DC and AC sides

Fitted in the DC and AC boxes, with a proper earth.

An inverter is electronics. Surge protection costs a small fraction of the inverter it saves.

Why we pick it

  • Thai storm seasons bring lightning surges down both the roof cables and the grid line. Protecting one side only leaves the inverter exposed.
  • Plug-in cartridges with a status window: you can see when one has done its job and needs replacing.
  • Installed close to the equipment with a short, direct earth connection, which is what makes the device effective.
  • Checked at every maintenance visit.

Automatic transfer switch (ATS)

An ATS rated for the full load, chosen per project

Used where a site has backup power, a generator or more than one supply.

The ATS is a safety device as much as a convenience. We size it to the site, not to a price list.

Why we pick it

  • Switches the load between sources by itself, so the lights come back without anyone going to the board.
  • Breaks one source before making the other, so solar or generator power can never feed back into the grid line.
  • Rated for the full current of the circuits it carries, with margin, not for the average load.
  • Laid out with a manual bypass so the site can still be run by hand during service.

Cable

LINK solar cable on the DC side, Yazaki cable on the AC side

Copper throughout, sized to keep losses low.

Cable is hidden once the job is finished, which is exactly why it is the first place cheap installations cut.

Why we pick it

  • Solar-rated DC cable is double insulated and built for years of sun, heat and rain on a roof.
  • Yazaki AC cable is made to Thai standards and is what inspectors expect to see.
  • We size cable by the length of the run, not by the minimum that will pass, so less of your solar power is lost as heat.
  • Run in conduit and wireway, clipped and labelled, with nothing left loose on the roof.
