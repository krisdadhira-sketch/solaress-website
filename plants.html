Plants by Province

Every plant Solar ESS installs or looks after has a code instead of a customer name. Plants are grouped here by province, so installers can see the work near them and owners can find the reports for their plant.

How to read a code: [Platform][Type]-[Postcode]-[Inverter kW]-[No.] – e.g. SG-52000-20-01 is a SolisCloud grid-tied plant in postcode area 52000 (Lampang) with a 20 kW inverter, the first such plant. The first two postcode digits give the province.
