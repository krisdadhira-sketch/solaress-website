What we check

The six problems we find most often when we review systems on SolisCloud, DeyeCloud, FusionSolar and other platforms.

ProductionkWh per kWp per day against what your roof should give: dirt, shading or a failed string show up here.
CT and meterDoes solar + grid − export + battery match your load? A wrong CT makes the app, the battery and zero export all wrong.
Battery settingsBattery held full and never used, reserve set too high or too low, time-of-use slots that buy from the grid.
Zero export and phasesSolar curtailed when it could be used, or one phase buying from the grid while another exports.
Alarms and offline loggersMonitoring gaps, a datalogger offline for weeks, or repeating fault codes.
Sizing for your billWhether more panels or a battery would actually lower your bill, based on your real daily curves.

What you get: real reports

This is the report we sent after a free check of a 6.48 kWp hybrid system with a 16 kWh battery in eastern Bangkok (customer details removed). The panels were fine; the battery reading was not, and the home was buying power from the grid before dawn while the battery still had energy.

  • Production checked against what the roof should give: ≈3.7 kWh/kWp/day, normal for Bangkok

Example 2: “My PEA bill shows more units than my app”

A 5.08 kWp hybrid system with battery in Pathum Thani. From April to October the PEA bill counted 4,458 units bought, while the inverter app recorded only 1,765 kWh. The panels were fine; the CT was reading the grid wrongly, so the battery and export control were working from wrong numbers.

  • Inverter app compared with every PEA bill, month by month (Apr–Oct)
  • A steady 2.5× gap in both directions (bought and sold) points to the CT, not hidden loads
  • Real October use worked out at ≈1,480 kWh, not the 1,189 kWh the app showed
  • A site checklist: CT position, direction, ratio setting and a clamp-meter comparison
