Thailand Solar Installer Alliance (TSIA)

Statement of Purpose

The Thailand Solar Installer Alliance (TSIA) is an independent, nationwide network of solar installers, electricians, engineers and renewable-energy advocates. We came together because the people who put solar on Thailand’s roofs deserve a community that shares knowledge freely, upholds high standards and stands behind its members.

Our visionEvery solar system in Thailand is designed and installed safely, correctly and to recognised standards, by trusted installers who are fairly rewarded for good work.

Our mission

1. Share knowledge, not sales pitches.We give installers an open forum to exchange technical know-how and solve installation engineering problems correctly and to standard. TSIA is a community for learning, not a sales channel.
2. Connect people with installers they can trust.We identify qualified, reliable installers in every region and pass work between members by recommendation, so customers are served by people the alliance vouches for.
3. Stand behind our members.We help resolve disputes arising from installation contracts and give solar installers access to legal advice, so no member faces a dispute alone.

Our principles

Safety and standards firstWe follow Thai electrical standards and manufacturers’ instructions on every job.
Honesty and transparencyClear scope, fair prices and honest advice, to customers and to each other.
Quality materials, qualified peopleGood components, properly matched, installed by trained and certified hands.
Knowledge is shared, not hoardedA problem solved by one member is a lesson for all.
Fairness on both sidesFair pay for installers and fair outcomes for customers.

Our goals

1. Technical expertise
  • Training for everyone involved in solar systems, from installers to designers and sales staff
  • Sharing new technologies and products as they reach the market
  • Raising installation standards across the country
  • Building a shared database of reliable suppliers
2. Marketing and branding
  • Marketing and branding support for members
  • Job sharing between members
  • A nationwide marketing network
  • A shared market database
3. Financial support
  • Access to credit terms
  • Financial planning advice
  • Tax and accounting support
  • Help with company registration
4. Legal support
  • Legal advice for members, including on contracts and disputes

Our commitment as members

Every TSIA member agrees to:

  • install to recognised standards, using qualified and certified electricians;
  • quote clearly, with scope, materials and price stated up front;
  • share lessons learned, including mistakes, so the whole alliance improves;
  • resolve disagreements in good faith and seek the alliance’s help early.
Our promise to Thailand. Through shared knowledge, trusted people and mutual support, TSIA works to make solar energy in Thailand safer, more reliable and more accessible, one well-installed system at a time.

Apply to join the Thailand Solar Installer Alliance

Installers, electrical contractors and solar companies anywhere in Thailand are welcome to apply. Tell us about your organisation, attach your company profile, registration and past work, and we will review your application against the alliance’s standards.

What members get

  • Training and technical exchange with experienced installers
  • Job sharing and recommendations to customers in your area
  • Marketing and branding support, and a shared supplier database
  • Help with credit terms, accounts, tax and company registration
  • Legal advice and support when a contract dispute arises

How we review applications

  • We check your registration and your past work
  • We may call you, visit a site or contact a past customer
  • Every member agrees to the TSIA Statement of Purpose and member commitments
  • We reply to every application by phone or email
