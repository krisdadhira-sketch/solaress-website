## 5. Warranty and warranty cost

The Contractor warrants its workmanship for 12 months from acceptance and pays all costs of fixing workmanship defects in that time.

| Item | Who pays | Terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workmanship defects (loose terminals, wrong wiring, leaks at roof fixings, structure, conduit, labelling) | Contractor | Free of charge: labour, travel and replacement parts damaged by the defect |
| Equipment failure (panels, inverter, battery, breakers) not caused by installation | Manufacturer, through Solar ESS | Contractor helps with diagnosis and replacement at a labour rate agreed in writing |
| Damage caused by misuse, accidents, natural disaster or work by others | Customer / insurer | Not covered by this warranty |

1. **Response time:** the Contractor answers a warranty call within 24 hours and is on site within 3 working days (urgent safety faults: within 24 hours where possible).
2. **Fix time:** the defect is fixed within 7 days of the visit, or a date agreed in writing.
3. If the Contractor does not respond or fix in time, Solar ESS fixes it with another team and deducts the cost from the retention or invoices the Contractor.
4. **Retention:** the 5% retention is released 12 months after acceptance, less any warranty costs, within 7 days of the end of the warranty.
